# Privacy Policy for SnapCalora AI

**Last Updated:** August 7, 2026

SnapCalora AI ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application (**SnapCalora AI**).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Account Information:** When you register or sign in, we collect your name and email address to manage your account and authenticate your sessions.
- **Nutritional & Fitness Data:** We collect the meal logs, dietary preferences, calorie and macronutrient counts, and daily step counts that you record in the app.
- **Photos & Media:** If you choose to use the AI Food Vision feature, the meal photos you select or capture are processed solely to analyze nutritional content and estimate calories/macros.
- **Device Information:** Standard diagnostic and crash data to ensure application stability.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Provide personalized calorie and macronutrient tracking.
- Analyze food images via AI to provide instant dietary feedback.
- Sync daily step counts and activity trends.
- Maintain and secure user accounts.

---

## 3. Data Protection & Security

- All communications between the SnapCalora AI mobile app and our servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data with third-party advertisers.
